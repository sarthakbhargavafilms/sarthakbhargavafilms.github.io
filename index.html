About the studio

One person. One machine.
Whole worlds.

Sarthak Bhargava is an independent filmmaker working where direction meets machine intelligence. Every film starts on the page — screenplay, shot list, blocking — before a single frame is generated.

Shots are rendered entirely in-studio, then held to the old standard: lighting, camera, mood, sound design, and edit rhythm. Generation is not direction — each shot is retaken until the frame behaves like cinema.

The tools are new. The discipline is a hundred years old.

STEP 01

Write

Screenplay, shot list, and blocking first. Story before pixels — always.

STEP 02

Generate

Shots rendered locally — no farms, no vendors, full control of every frame.

STEP 03

Direct

Light, lens, camera move, and cut — retaken until it behaves like cinema.

STEP 04

Score

Original music and professional foley — a frame without sound is half a frame.
